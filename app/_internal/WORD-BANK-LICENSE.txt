The original hand-written word bank is retained. Added entries were selected
using wordfreq 3.1.1 frequency data by Robyn Speer and normalized with Russian
OpenCorpora dictionary data distributed as pymorphy3-dicts-ru
2.4.417150.4580142. Selection excludes existing words, abbreviations,
several nonstandard tags, explicit vocabulary, and obvious word fragments.
Frequencies of noun inflections were summed by lemma; at most 150 entries per initial letter in the first stage and at most
500 additional entries in the latest stage were retained above the same cutoff.
